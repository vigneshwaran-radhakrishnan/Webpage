Biography

A researcher who works on the design and integrity of aerospace structures, currently focusing mainly on ductile failure processes. The demands and challenges in the field are continuously evolving, thrusting our imaginations to a new level. Since my undergraduate studies, my interests were closely aligned with structural mechanics, I am continuing to do that throughout my professional career. My experience as an Engineer in VEM Technologies (An Aerospace and Defense company) helped in learning the life cycle of a product from design to delivery. Keeping aside other technical details such as what I do, how I do, I like flying airplanes and playing volleyball. Ping me when you near Texas, I will take you for a ride ;)
