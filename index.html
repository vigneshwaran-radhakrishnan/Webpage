About Me

My research lies at the intersection between solid mechanics and the physics of plasticity, damage and fracture with applications to grand challenge problems in manufacturing as well as in the performance / damage tolerance of materials and structures under the extreme environments of power generation systems, deep space exploration, aging aircraft, hypersonics, etc. I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher working with Prof. Pradeep Sharma at the University of Houston. I earned my Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering from Texas A&M University in May 2024.

My doctoral research focused on the computational modeling of ductile failure in lightweight metals. I am now extending that work to develop a machine learning-based damage model for large-scale structural simulations. I have an industry experience as an Engineer at VEM Technologies (an Aerospace and Defense company) where I learned the life cycle of a product from design to delivery.

Aside from research, I am a certified private pilot and enjoy playing volleyball and cricket!
